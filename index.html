03 — How We Work

Four moves, no noise

  1. 01

    Brief

    A ninety-minute conversation about how you actually live — not a form. We leave with a written brief you approve.

  2. 02

    Curate

    We shortlist from on-market, off-market and pre-market inventory. You see six properties, not sixty.

  3. 03

    Negotiate

    Independent appraisal, structural review and a negotiation strategy built on data rather than instinct.

  4. 04

    Settle

    We coordinate legal, finance and trades so that closing is an administrative footnote, not an event.
